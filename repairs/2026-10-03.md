## Operations

- `BILIBILI_COOKIE` is optional login state, not a Discord webhook. Never paste it into public chat, files, or logs. It cannot guarantee removal of upstream anti-bot restrictions.
- Cookies are domain-scoped to Bilibili; the same session must not leak a raw Cookie header to Anime1/YouTube.
- Partial scans remain retryable and fail the workflow visibly, while `always()` saves confirmed deliveries.
- Seven-day alerts distinguish failed source checks from a successful scan that found no new episodes. Completed seasons do not alert.
- Known limitations: recent uploader API pages and Telegram/RSS feeds have bounded history. A long outage or a completely new uploader still requires reviewed source replacement; no arbitrary search hit is automatically posted.
- Delivery receipts prevent routine retry duplicates. A network failure after Discord accepted a POST but before returning its receipt is inherently ambiguous and requires message reconciliation; this is not an exactly-once delivery guarantee.
